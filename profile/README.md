🧠 Vision
We believe the future of quantitative trading is autonomous. 7N7D is building an ecosystem of AI agents that can:
- Research market inefficiencies and structural edges.
- Generate strategy code in multiple languages (Python, Solidity, TypeScript).
- Backtest against historical and simulated on-chain data.
- Deploy execution and settlement layers with minimal human oversight.

Owlpha is our flagship agent — designed to learn, iterate, and improve trading strategies continuously.
